NextLeap Top PM Fellow capstone

Spotify Discovery Compass

A discovery product for listeners stuck on repeat: an editable session intent contract before ranking, and a visible steering loop after every recommendation set.

Problem
Discovery is neither context aware nor user controlled: 75.6% of surveyed listeners replay the same songs and 51.2% are dissatisfied with recommendations.
Solution
Mined 1,850 App Store, Play Store, and Reddit records with a RAG-assisted, citation-backed method, then prioritised three concepts with ICE before designing the Compass flow and wireframes.
Impact
North Star of weekly qualified discovery actions per active user, backed by leading, lagging, and guardrail metrics plus a rollout and monetisation plan.
